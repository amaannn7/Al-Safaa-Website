# Questions to ask before building

Ask the ones whose answer is not already in the brief. Most are cheap to ask and
expensive to get wrong; each maps to a correction a client actually made on the
reference build. Group them in one message with a recommended default for each,
so the client can answer "yes to all" and move on.

## Content and truth
- **Fidelity.** Should the new site say only what the old site said (recommended), or may I write new copy? If new copy: who approves it?
- **Conflicts.** I found two addresses / two WhatsApp numbers / two palettes in your own material. Which is right?  (Offer your best-supported pick as the default.)
- **Claims.** Are the headline claims still true and may we publish them (founded year, number of customers, "5-star rated", awards)?
- **Reviews.** Where are the real reviews? Can you paste or screenshot 4 to 6 you are happy to show, with the reviewer's name? Which rating and review count should show?
- **Services.** Which secondary services are offered on their own (visa, hotels, transport, extended stays), as opposed to only inside packages?
- **Legal.** Is there a licence/registration number to show? Who reviews the privacy policy?

## Scope and rules
- **Forbidden features.** Anything that must not appear? (booking, checkout, login, forms, prices, embedded third-party widgets).  Treat look-alikes as forbidden too.
- **Contact channels.** Which is primary (WhatsApp, phone, email) and which number goes on the floating button?
- **Style rules.** Any banned punctuation (em dashes), spelling variant (US/UK), or words to avoid?

## Design
- **Reference.** Is there a design you like? Send the link or screenshots; I will take its typography and density from it.
- **Fonts and colours.** Any fonts to avoid ("too common") or brand colours that differ from the logo?
- **Existing assets to keep exactly.** Logo, favicon, posters, photos. (If the answer is "keep the old one", restore the original file, do not rebuild a lookalike.)
- **Imagery.** Do you have your own photos (groups, hotels, offices, team)? Who owns the existing ones? May stock photography be used?

## Launch
- **Hosting and domain.** Vercel? Who controls DNS? Is email on the same provider (do not touch MX records)?
- **Posters/flyers** printed with old details: will updated artwork be supplied?
- **Date-driven content.** Which items expire (tours, offers, events) and what should the page show when nothing is upcoming?
